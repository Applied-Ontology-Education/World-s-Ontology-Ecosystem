# Final Audit

Write approximately 300–500 words.

## LLM-only baseline

What did the model claim without an ontology, and did it disclose the missing
evidence?

## Ontology grounding

Which claims became traceable to active axiom IDs? Identify any unsupported
step that remained.

## Critic contribution

What did the Critic improve? What could it not formally guarantee?

## SHACL feedback

Describe one violation and one conforming arrangement. Explain why conformance
establishes possibility rather than entailment.

## Belief revision

4?

## Agent–reasoner comparison

not?
