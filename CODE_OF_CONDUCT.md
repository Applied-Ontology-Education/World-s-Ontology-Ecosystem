# Code of Conduct

This course welcomes participants with widely different levels of experience. Productive disagreement is expected; contempt for beginners or for competing modeling positions is not.

Participants should:

- criticize claims, axioms, and methods rather than people;
- distinguish errors from defensible alternatives;
- identify assumptions and practical consequences;
- avoid sharing classified, controlled, proprietary, or personally identifying information;
- use fictional or approved data in all public submissions;
- give credit when adapting another team's work.

Organizers may remove content or participation that disrupts the course, exposes restricted information, or targets other participants.
